Support

Nestis, a fasting timer for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Need help with Nestis? We usually reply within a day or two.

Email hello@nestis.app

How do I sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Nestis syncs automatically through iCloud. Make sure you're signed into the same iCloud account on both devices, with iCloud Drive enabled in Settings > [your name] > iCloud.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Nestis, go to Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your device, or tap Manage subscription inside Nestis's Settings screen.

How do I export my data?

Nestis Pro can export your full fasting history as JSON, PDF, or CSV from Settings > Export data. Importing a backup is free for everyone.

Something's not working. What do I do?

Email us at hello@nestis.app with a description of the issue and, if possible, your device model and iOS version. Screenshots help a lot.
